Continuous Wavelet Transform

W(a,b) = (1/√a) ∫ f(t)ψ*((t-b)/a) dt

a: scale parameter
b: translation parameter
ψ: mother wavelet

Top: Original Signal
Middle: Scalogram (time-scale representation)
Bottom: Wavelet decomposition levels

Wavelets provide simultaneous time and frequency localization, unlike Fourier transforms.
